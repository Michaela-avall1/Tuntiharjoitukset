What to Grow

Choosing the right plants for your garden is crucial for success. Get inspired by these easy-to-grow options.

Simple Plants to Start With

Potatoes

Potatoes are Tessa's favorite choice for beginners to grow. Easy and rewarding!

Carrots

Carrots are easy to grow and also perfect for beginners. They need well-drained soil.

Tomatoes

Tomatoes are easy to grow even on a balcony. They need full sun and regular watering.

Lettuce

Lettuce is a garden favorite. It needs cool temperatures and consistent moisture.

Herbs

Herbs are easy to grow and they are a great addition to any garden.

Radishes

Radishes are quick to grow so you can enjoy them in no time.
